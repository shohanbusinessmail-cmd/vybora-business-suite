# Brand Identity — Vybora Business Suite

## 1. Name
- English: **Vybora Business Suite**
- Bengali: **ভাইবোরা বিজনেস স্যুট**
- Short: **Vybora** · Pronunciation: vy-BOR-ah
- Tagline (bn): "আপনার ব্যবসার নির্ভরযোগ্য হিসাবরক্ষক" (Your business's reliable accountant)
- Rationale: matches the owner-created repository; short, distinctive, pronounceable
  in Bengali and English, commerce-neutral for future expansion (ADR-002).

## 2. Naming research (2026-11-02) and limitations
Web search for "Vybora" found small unrelated GitHub projects (an entertainment
discovery demo, a social-app demo). No established business-management/POS brand or
obvious trademark conflict appeared in searched results. Domain availability not
exhaustively verified. **This is not trademark clearance.** The owner retains final
legal responsibility for trademark and domain clearance before commercial launch.

## 3. Visual identity
- Logo: original geometric mark — a stylized "V" formed by two ascending bars (growth)
  inside a rounded square, deep green with a gold accent node. SVG source + PNG
  renders + multi-size Windows ICO (16/24/32/48/64/128/256).
- Colors: primary #0F6B4F, primary-dark #0B523D, accent gold #C9A227, neutrals per
  UI_DESIGN_SPEC.md. Light mode only.
- Typography: Noto Sans Bengali (OFL 1.1) for Bengali + Latin; bundled in assets/fonts.
- Iconography: original line icons, 1.75px stroke, rounded caps.
- Splash: logo + product name (bn) + version + "লোড হচ্ছে…".

## 4. Asset license inventory
See ASSET_LICENSES.md. All brand assets original (project-owned); font OFL-1.1;
no third-party logos or unclear-license assets used.

## 5. Usage rules
Minimum logo size 24px; clear space = ½ mark height; never recolor outside palette;
never stretch; Bengali name always in Noto Sans Bengali; installer/app icon identical.
